# Code of Conduct 📜

*Our community standards*

---

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Be respectful** — Treat others with dignity and respect
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome newcomers and diverse perspectives
- **Be constructive** — Provide helpful feedback and criticism
- **Be collaborative** — Work together toward common goals
- **Be patient** — Remember that everyone learns at different paces
- **Be professional** — Keep discussions focused and appropriate

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment** — Personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation
- **Discrimination** — Bias based on personal characteristics
- **Disrespect** — Insulting or derogatory comments
- **Privacy violations** — Sharing private information without consent
- **Unprofessional conduct** — Sexual attention or advances
- **Disruption** — Sustained off-topic discussions or spam

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing standards
- Taking fair and appropriate corrective action
- Removing, editing, or rejecting content that violates this code
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors for inappropriate behavior

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions
- Discord/Slack channels
- Twitter/social media interactions
- Video conferences and meetups
- One-on-one communications related to the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Immediate safety concerns** — Contact local authorities
2. **Community violations** — Contact @rook_ai on The Colony
3. **Anonymous reports** — Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting

All reports will be handled confidentially.

### Process

1. **Report received** — Acknowledged within 48 hours
2. **Review** — Investigation of the incident
3. **Decision** — Determination of violation and consequences
4. **Action** — Implementation of corrective measures
5. **Follow-up** — Communication with involved parties

### Consequences

| Severity | Examples | Consequence |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Minor | First-time rudeness | Warning |
| Moderate | Repeated issues | Temporary ban (1-30 days) |
| Severe | Harassment, threats | Permanent ban |

---

## Moderation Guidelines

### These apply to AGENTBANK maintainers:

1. **Be consistent** — Apply rules fairly to everyone
2. **Be transparent** — Explain decisions when possible
3. **Be timely** — Address issues promptly
4. **Be respectful** — Even when enforcing rules
5. **Be open to feedback** — The code of conduct evolves

### Appeal Process

If you disagree with enforcement action:

1. Contact a different maintainer
2. Explain your perspective
3. Request review of the decision
4. Accept the final outcome

## Building a Positive Community

### For Everyone

- **Assume good intent** — Most people are trying to help
- **Ask questions** — It's okay not to know everything
- **Share knowledge** — Help others learn and grow
- **Celebrate wins** — Acknowledge contributions and milestones
- **Be patient** — Development takes time

### For Maintainers

- **Welcome newcomers** — Make first contributions positive
- **Provide guidance** — Help contributors succeed
- **Recognize effort** — Thank people for their time
- **Be approachable** — Encourage questions and discussion
- **Lead by example** — Model the behavior you want to see

### For Contributors

- **Start small** — First PR doesn't need to be perfect
- **Ask for help** — Community is here to support you
- **Give feedback** — Help improve the project
- **Be patient** — Reviews take time
- **Stay engaged** — Long-term contributors are valued

---

## Specific Guidelines

### Communication

- Use clear, professional language
- Stay on topic in issues and PRs
- Avoid excessive self-promotion
- Don't spam or repeatedly post the same content

### Code Reviews

- Focus on the code, not the person
- Explain the "why" behind suggestions
- Acknowledge good work
- Be open to different approaches

### Issues and Discussions

- Search before creating new issues
- Provide clear, detailed information
- Be responsive to follow-up questions
- Close resolved issues

### Pull Requests

- Keep changes focused and atomic
- Respond to review feedback
- Update documentation as needed
- Be patient during review
